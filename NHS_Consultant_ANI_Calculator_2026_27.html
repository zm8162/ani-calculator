Assumptions & data ▼
  • Tax year: 2026/27 only.
  • Consultant pay source: 2026/27 NHS Employers Pay and Conditions Circular (M&D) 1/2026 R2.
  • Programmed activities: One PA equals one tenth of nodal-point basic salary. First 10 PAs are pensionable; additional PAs are non-pensionable.
  • Pension contributions: Rates and payslip override are estimates.
  • EV benefit-in-kind: Calculated at 4% for zero-emission cars in 2026/27. P11D figures are estimates and should be checked where precision matters.
  • ANI treatment: Pension contributions, EV BIK, professional fees, SIPP and Gift Aid are treated as specified.
  • Other income: Dividends and non-ISA interest are included in ANI; ISA income is excluded.
  • Professional fee defaults: GMC £481, RCoA £599, MPS £549 and BMA £46/month.
  • Pro-rata salary: If an increment date falls within the 2026/27 tax year (between 1 April 2026 and 31 March 2027), salary, on-call supplement, and pensionable pay are split pro-rata across the two pay points.

Disclaimer: This is an estimation tool, not regulated financial/tax advice. Check your payslip and HMRC/NHSBSA records where exact figures matter.
